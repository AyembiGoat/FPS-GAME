### Default Active Loadout & Switching

- **Primary Slot (`1`)**: Defaults to **HK416** (can equip any of the 6 Primaries: HK416, AK-47, SCAR-H, MP5, Remington 870, AWM).
- **Secondary Slot (`2`)**: Defaults to **M1911** (can equip either Pistol: M1911 or Desert Eagle).
- **Switching**: Press `1` or `2` (or mouse wheel scroll if enabled in Settings) to swap cleanly between Primary and Secondary with a snappy `0.34s` holster/draw transition (`switchRemaining` in core `WeaponState`). Players can also change equipped Primary or Secondary anytime from the **Loadout** screen (Title or Pause), persisted in `localStorage` (`fps.loadout.v1`).
- **Shotgun Multi-Pellet Deterministic Spread (`src/core`)**: `stepGame` computes deterministic radial-ring + jitter offsets from a pure shot counter (`shotsFired`), firing 8 pellets per trigger pull (1 dead-center core pellet + 7 evenly distributed ring pellets with deterministic per-shot angular twist, tightened when ADS). Every pellet raycasts independently, producing multiple bullet holes/dust puffs and knocking down targets hit by any pellet, while counting as 1 shot fired and at most 1 hit per target for clean run accuracy telemetry.

### Weapon Audio Profiles (`src/game/audio/sounds.ts`)

Each firearm class uses a distinct synthesised acoustic recipe routed to the `weapon` bus (keeping all handling/reload sounds `< 0.2` of the gunshot reference peak):

- **HK416 (`5.56`)**: High-velocity sharp supersonic crack (`3.8 kHz`), tight snappy mid bark (`210→70 Hz`), crisp bolt clack.
- **AK-47 (`7.62×39`)**: Deeper punchy mid-low bark (`175→52 Hz`), heavier metallic bolt carrier ring, fuller dust-cover clack.
- **SCAR-H (`7.62×51`)**: Heavy battle-rifle concussion (`145→44 Hz`), crisp high-pressure crack, authoritative piston cycle.
- **MP5 (`9mm`)**: Compact high-cadence subsonic/supersonic pop (`250→95 Hz`), shorter tail, rapid roller-lock bolt click.
- **Remington 870 (`12 Gauge`)**: Massive low-end booming thump (`110→34 Hz`) + wide blast noise, followed by a delayed two-beat pump forend **chk-chk** rack at `+0.26s` / `+0.44s`!
- **AWM (`.338 Lapua`)**: Thunderous cannon crack + deep sub-chest shockwave (`95→28 Hz`, longer decay), followed by a crisp metallic bolt lift/pull/push cycle at `+0.38s` / `+0.62s` / `+0.84s`!
- **M1911 (`.45 ACP`)**: Existing classic punchy pistol crack + slide clack (`165→48 Hz`).
- **Desert Eagle (`.50 AE`)**: Heavy booming hand-cannon report (`128→38 Hz`) with long crack and heavy steel slide slam.

## 3. Architectural Plan for Phases 2–6

### Phase 2: Pure Core + Procedural 3D Weapon Models

- **`src/core/weapon.ts`**: Define `WeaponId`, `WeaponCategory`, `WeaponSlot`, `FireMode`, `ScopeStyle`, `WeaponSpec`, `WEAPONS` registry, `DEFAULT_LOADOUT`, `LoadoutState`, and extend `WeaponState` with `activeSlot`, `slots: { primary: SlotWeaponState; secondary: SlotWeaponState }`, `switchRemaining`, and `shotSequence` (deterministic counter for pellet spread). Support `semi`, `auto`, `pump`, and `bolt` fire modes, clean slot switching (`1`/`2` or `switchSlot` input), and `loadout` reconfiguration while preserving full determinism.
- **`src/core/game.ts` & `src/core/bot.ts`**: Update `stepGame` to fire active weapon (single ray or multi-pellet shotgun spread), emit weapon-tagged `shot` events (supporting multi-hit pellets cleanly), and teach `createBot` to use all weapon types (adjusting engagement distance for shotgun, waiting for full ADS on sniper/rifles, firing semi/auto/pump/bolt cleanly).
- **`src/game/materials/recipes.ts`**: Add authentic firearm & range material recipes to the shared library:
  - `'gun-anodized'` (matte black tactical aluminum for HK416 / MP5 / optic bodies)
  - `'gun-polymer'` (fine stippled matte black polymer for stocks, rails, grips, MP5/Rem870 furniture)
  - `'gun-fde'` (Flat Dark Earth matte anodized alloy for SCAR-H upper)
  - `'gun-fde-polymer'` (coyote tan textured polymer for SCAR-H lower/stock)
  - `'gun-olive'` (Accuracy International olive-drab chassis polymer for AWM)
  - `'gun-stainless'` (brushed satin stainless steel for Desert Eagle & bolt carriers)
  - `'ak-wood'` (warm varnished Russian laminated birch/amber hardwood for AK-47 stock & handguard)
  - `'steel-container'` (weathered dark maritime slate-blue corrugated steel for Shoot House shipping containers)
